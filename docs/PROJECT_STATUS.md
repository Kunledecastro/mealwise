## Completed
- Phases 0–2 (approved).
- Phase 3 so far:
  - 93 ingredients with nutrition generated by script from FAO/INFOODS West Africa 2019 and USDA FoodData Central; every figure traceable to a source code.
  - 40 meals (Nigerian, Ghanaian, International, Asian, Mediterranean) with recipes, gram amounts, steps and written assumptions. Calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, allergens and diet labels are calculated, not typed.
  - Recommendation engine: allergy/diet/dislike/"Not for me" rules, then ranking by goal, time, budget, cuisine, variety and saved meals, with plain-language reasons.
  - Every screen now uses the real catalogue (Home, Meal ideas, Search, Saved, meal pages, Today, landing page).
  - "Saved" and "Not for me" saved to your account (new table, owner-only).
  - Quarterly price sheet and importer; catalogue review report.
  - From your recipe files (2026-10-19): 6 dishes from the Nigeria eCookbook (coconut rice, ofe onugbu, afang, pate, margi special, tuwo and miyan kuka) and 4 from the others (lentils with rice, cucumber salad, cabbage slaw, fruit smoothie). Steps in our own words with "Based on" credits.
  - "Cook it or buy it" on every meal page. 18 classic buka dishes show a typical Lagos price of ₦1,500–4,500 a plate, dated and sourced, with no vendor names. Cards show the buy price when the cooking cost isn't known, and budget ranking uses it.
