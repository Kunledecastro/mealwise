## Completed
- Phases 0–2 (approved).
- Phase 3:
  - **70 meals**:
    - 17 breakfast, 47 lunch, 47 dinner, 14 snack options.
    - 28 vegetarian, 11 vegan.
    - Nigerian, Ghanaian, International, Asian, Mediterranean.
    - Includes 10 dishes from your recipe files, credited.
  - **97 ingredients** generated by script from FAO/INFOODS West Africa 2019 and USDA FoodData Central. Calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, allergens and diet labels are calculated, never typed.
  - **Recommendation engine:** allergy (including "may contain" and unknown), diet, dislike and "Not for me" rules. Ranking by goal, time, budget, cuisine, variety and saved meals, with reasons.
  - "Saved" and "Not for me" stored in your account.
  - **Cook it or buy it**:
    - Cooking cost from NBS (May 2026) where every ingredient is priced: 14 meals.
    - Typical Lagos buka price for 21 dishes.
    - Together, 34 meals show some price.
  - Price sheets and importers for quarterly updates; catalogue review report.
