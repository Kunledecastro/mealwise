## Completed
- Phases 0–2 (approved).
- Phase 3 batch 1:
  - 70 ingredients with nutrition generated by script from FAO/INFOODS West Africa 2019 and USDA FoodData Central; every figure traceable to a source code.
  - 40 meals (Nigerian, Ghanaian, International, Asian, Mediterranean) with recipes, gram amounts, steps and written assumptions. Calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, allergens and diet labels are calculated, not typed.
  - Recommendation engine: allergy/diet/dislike/"Not for me" rules, then ranking by goal, time, budget, cuisine, variety and saved meals, with plain-language reasons.
  - Every screen now uses the real catalogue (Home, Meal ideas, Search, Saved, meal pages, Today, landing page).
  - "Saved" and "Not for me" saved to your account (new table, owner-only).
  - Quarterly price sheet and importer; catalogue review report.
